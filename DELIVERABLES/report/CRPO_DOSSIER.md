---
## 1. The one-sentence claim (core novelty)

> **In iterative LLM self-repair, the model's own failed program is an *endogenous confounder*: it is the source of the
> useful verifier evidence, yet its direct presence in the next context *anchors* the policy back toward the failed
> hypothesis. We quantify this with a causal metric (the Causal Signal Ratio) and show that a *proposal-invariant,
> verifier-sufficient* state — keep the counterexample, erase the failed code — decontaminates repair.**

Nobody in the counterexample-guided-repair literature (CEGIS, MENTOR, ExVerus) treats the failed proposal as a *causal
nuisance variable whose direct effect should be interventionally removed*. That reframing is the contribution.

---
## 2. The theory being tested

Self-repair state is a triple **s = (q, P, E)**: task `q`, failed proposal `P` (the model's previous program), verifier
evidence `E` (a concrete counterexample: failing input → expected vs got). There are two causal paths from `P` to the
next repair `Y`:

```
        (useful, mediated)              (harmful, direct)
   P ────────────► E ────────► Y        P ───────────────► Y
   the failed code produces the         the failed code, sitting in the
   counterexample that teaches           context, biases the next policy
   the fix                               back toward the same wrong idea
```

- **Mediated effect P→E→Y** is what we want to keep: the counterexample carries the information needed to fix the bug.
- **Direct effect P→Y** is anchoring/contamination: seeing its own failed code makes the model re-commit to it.

**Central, testable prediction (counterintuitive):** *more state information can lower policy quality when that
information is generated by the policy itself.* For a Bayes-optimal learner, adding `P` to `(q,E)` cannot hurt. For a
finite LLM policy it can — because `P` injects the direct anchoring path. Formally we expect the repair value ordering
`J(q,E) > J(q,P,E)` and, at the distribution level, that the policy is **more sensitive to `P` than to `E`** under
full-history repair.

### The metrics (all measured on *verifier-observable* behavior — the distribution over per-test pass-vectors of K
sampled repairs — so they are objective, not judged):

- **Proposal Direct Effect** `DE_P = D_JS( π(Y|q,E,P_A) ‖ π(Y|q,E,P_B) )` — hold evidence fixed, swap the failed
  proposal. Pure contamination. **Want → 0.**
- **Evidence Effect** `IE_E = D_JS( π(Y|q,P,E₁) ‖ π(Y|q,P,E₂) )` — hold proposal fixed, change the evidence. Pure
  signal. **Want ≫ 0.**
- **Causal Signal Ratio** `CSR = IE_E / DE_P`. A good repair policy listens to evidence over its own past guess:
  `CSR ≫ 1`. An anchored full-history policy: `CSR ≪ 1`.

Two context constructions of the *same* base model isolate the effect: **RAW** (repair prompt shows failed code + E)
vs **EVID** (shows only E, code hidden = "Forget to Repair"). Prediction: RAW has large `DE_P`; EVID zeroes `DE_P` by
construction *without* sacrificing `IE_E`.

## 3. CORE RESULT — landed (causal matrix, 3 families, MBPP)

`causal_matrix.py`, base models, MBPP, K=8 repairs/condition. JSONs death-proofed to `runs_pulled/crpo/causal_matrix/`.

| family | n_cases | DE_P raw | DE_P evid | IE_E raw | IE_E evid | **CSR raw** | solve raw | solve evid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct | 199 | 0.245 | 0.000 | 0.098 | 0.107 | **0.40** | 0.290 | **0.458** (+0.168) |
| Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct | 209 | 0.188 | 0.000 | 0.108 | 0.133 | **0.57** | 0.200 | **0.283** (+0.083) |
| Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct | 310 | 0.177 | 0.000 | 0.102 | 0.125 | **0.58** | 0.209 | **0.294** (+0.085) |

**Reading:**
1. **CSR < 1 on every family (0.40–0.58).** Under full-history repair the failed proposal shifts the next repair
   distribution *more* than the verifier evidence does. The theory's central prediction holds.
2. **The strongest model is the most contaminated** (Qwen-Coder CSR 0.40) — capability does not immunize against
   self-anchoring; if anything a confident coder clings harder to its own code.
3. **Erasing the proposal is strictly good:** `DE_P → 0` (by construction), `IE_E` *rises* (0.098→0.107, 0.108→0.133,
   0.102→0.125 — evidence-only is *more* evidence-sensitive), and controlled repair-solve improves **+8.3 to +16.8 pts**
   (same problem, same evidence, code shown vs hidden).

This complements the two prior inference results: §8 leakage geometry (failed code carries ~all proposal identity,
L≈1.0, vs certificate ≈0) and §10 CEGIS recovery (counterexample-only > code+counterexample). The matrix adds the
**policy-level causal metric** those two implied.

## 4. CORE RESULT #2 — self-anchoring is proposal-specific (adversarial injection)

`adv_inject.py`: hold the true evidence `E` fixed; vary the proposal shown — the model's OWN failed code, a DIFFERENT
problem's failed code (`other`), a DIFFERENT problem's PASSING code (`adv`, authoritative-looking but wrong here), or
nothing (`evid`). Repair success@8.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen-Coder (instF) | 296 | **0.662** | 0.784 | 0.801 | 0.801 | +0.139 |
| Qwen-Coder (instG replicate) | 285 | **0.653** | 0.786 | 0.796 | 0.814 | +0.161 |

**Reading (honest reframing):** the "poisoning" hypothesis — that an injected *adversarial* proposal is the most harmful

is uniquely harmful** (worst on every family, own→evid gap +0.085 to +0.206), *more* harmful than a random foreign wrong
program. Anchoring is specific to *self*-history, not to bad context in general. Evidence-only is best/tied-best. The
Qwen-Coder replicate (0.653 vs 0.662 own; 0.814 vs 0.801 evid) confirms tight reproducibility.

## 9. Honest paper position

The load-bearing contribution is an **inference-time + mechanism** result, now with a causal metric:

> *Forget to Repair: the failed proposal contaminates verifier-guided self-correction. Across three model families the
> Causal Signal Ratio of full-history repair is < 1 — the model's own failed program influences the next repair more
> than the verifier evidence does — and it is the model's* own *proposal specifically (not bad context in general) that
> anchors it. Erasing the proposal while keeping the counterexample removes the contamination, increases evidence
> sensitivity, and improves repair, at inference and as a deployment loop.*

CRPO *training* and Quotient-GRPO are framed as the natural method built on this measurement and pursued as upside — not
as a claimed capability win, because our own matched-compute RL comparisons (§17b) returned capability parity. The
strength here is the honest, reproducible, three-family *mechanism*, not another GRPO reward tweak.
