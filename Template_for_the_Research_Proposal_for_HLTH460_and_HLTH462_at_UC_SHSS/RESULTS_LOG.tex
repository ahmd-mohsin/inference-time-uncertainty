\section{Ablations ($\S$171/177)}
\textbf{Data efficiency:} $\sim\!25$ verified pairs capture the full gain ($\Delta+.108$@25 vs $+.112$@800).
\textbf{One-shot:} iterating RVP collapses it ($-.118$ MATH-500, $-.154$ Omni) --- single-pass is optimal.
\textbf{Hard-neg mining:} statistical tie with random verified negatives ($\Delta\le\pm.006$).
\textbf{$\beta$-robustness:} net-positive for $\beta\in\{.1,.2,.5\}$ all datasets; only $\beta{=}.03$
over-optimizes. \textbf{RVP-from-base:} reaches only $.24$--$.28$ vs $.62$--$.65$ from RFT $\Rightarrow$ the
RFT coverage stage is necessary.

\section{Scope boundaries + honest retractions}
Wins: math-specialized base \emph{and} instruct. Boundaries: general base mixed; RLHF-chat (Yi) collapses;

withdrew a confounded RVP-from-base null (over-optimized DPO); $\S$176 corrected the hard-neg ``HURTS''
artifact to a tie. \texttt{competition\_math} loader dead upstream $\Rightarrow$ dropped, not guessed.

\section{Theory corrections (Astra review, $\S$182)}
Prop.~1 rescoped: positive-only CE \emph{does} lower $\log\pi(y^-)$ diffusely via the softmax normaliser
($\partial\log\pi(y^+)/\partial z_{y^-}=-\pi(y^-)$); the honest claim is \emph{untargeted} suppression,
empirically dominated by the shared-feature lift $\Rightarrow$ net margin flat (scoped-empirical, not
impossibility). Cor.~1.1 softened to a finite-budget/signal statement (GRPO $\approx$ base empirically, not
a theorem that outcome-RL can never acquire hard prompts). Contribution reframed: large headroom-gated
selection gain + amortization + \emph{predictive} scope, not ``verified negatives uniquely necessary.''
